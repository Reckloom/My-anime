Privacy, plainly

This page talks directly to AniList from your browser.

Your AniList token is stored in this browser only. It is not sent to FRAME, ChatGPT, or another server. The permission is powerful because AniList does not offer narrower list-edit scopes. Disconnect removes the saved token from this browser.

The ChatGPT link only carries a media ID, media type, status, and optional progress/score — never your token.

Important: this is a companion bridge, not a native ChatGPT plugin. ChatGPT can prepare a link, and this page applies the change after you review it. The browser must be connected to AniList first.
